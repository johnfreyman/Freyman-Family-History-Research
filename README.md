# Freyman Family History — Public Research Mirror

This is the **public research workspace** for the Freyman / Freymann family-history project.

The private master archive contains original family documents and is the canonical preservation repository. This public mirror intentionally contains **research notes, evidence summaries, source references, open questions, and AI research assignments**, but not private original family documents.

## Research focus

The project currently follows the family through Riga and Latvia, the Russian Far East and China Sea, Baltic German resettlement, Danzig/Gotenhafen, wartime and postwar Germany, maritime business history, and eventual immigration to North America.

## Evidence labels

- **DOCUMENTED** — supported by a contemporary record or archival/documentary source.
- **PRIMARY FAMILY SOURCE** — stated by George Freymann, Ingrid Deinhardt Freymann, or another firsthand family source.
- **CORROBORATED** — independently supported by multiple underlying sources.
- **PROBABLE** — strong evidence but not conclusively proved.
- **HYPOTHESIS** — plausible research lead requiring verification.
- **DISPROVEN** — an earlier claim contradicted by better evidence.

AI agreement is not corroboration. Corroboration requires independent evidence.
