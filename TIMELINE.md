# Family Timeline

> Public working chronology. Entries identify current evidence status. Later-generation personal details are minimized in this public mirror.

## 19th century

- **Before 1862** — Georg Freymann is described by grandson George as a master machinist; he later works at the Waldschloesschen brewery in Riga. **PRIMARY FAMILY SOURCE**
- **1862** — Georg Freymann marries Marie Jaeger, according to George's memoir. **PRIMARY FAMILY SOURCE**
- **24 Nov 1874** — Johannes Ferdinand Freymann born in Riga. **DOCUMENTED / CORROBORATED in earlier research**

## 1900s–1910s — Russian Far East

- **June 1908** — Johannes/Hannes returns to Riga from Siberia by the Siberian Railroad, according to George. **PRIMARY FAMILY SOURCE**
- **c. 1908** — Johannes marries Alice Brand of Riga; exact wedding date still to be verified. **PRIMARY FAMILY SOURCE**
- **After marriage** — Johannes and Alice travel across Russia and Siberia to Iman, where Johannes is associated with a brewery. **PRIMARY FAMILY SOURCE**
- **20 Sep 1909 Julian / 3 Oct 1909 Gregorian** — George E. Freymann born at Iman. **PRIMARY FAMILY SOURCE / CORROBORATED**
- **13 Oct 1911** — Ingrid Deinhardt born in Riga. **DOCUMENTED in a 1939 Latvian government repatriation notice**
- **c. 1911** — George's younger brother “Vanitschka” born. **PRIMARY FAMILY SOURCE**
- **Childhood** — Vanitschka dies during an epidemic. **PRIMARY FAMILY SOURCE**

## 1930s — Riga

- **4 Jun 1932** — George Freymann marries Ingrid Deinhardt in Riga. **PRIMARY FAMILY SOURCE; documentary verification pending**
- **1930s** — George develops his shipping career in Latvia. **PRIMARY FAMILY SOURCE**

## 1939 — Baltic German resettlement

- **End of Nov 1939** — Ingrid, her children, George's mother-in-law, a governess and others in the household leave Latvia aboard the liner *Gneisenau*. **PRIMARY FAMILY SOURCE**
- **15 Dec 1939** — George arrives in Gdynia aboard *Der Deutsche*. **PRIMARY FAMILY SOURCE**

## 1941

- **16 Aug 1941** — Johannes Ferdinand Freymann dies in Gotenhafen (present-day Gdynia). **DOCUMENTED / CORROBORATED**
- **17 Sep 1941** — Contemporary death notice describes Johann Freymann as a captain and co-owner of the Latvian Shipping Co. **DOCUMENTED in earlier research**

## 1980s–1990

- **4 Jun 1982** — George and Ingrid's Golden Wedding Anniversary; the memoir project was substantially developed around this period. **PRIMARY FAMILY SOURCE**
- **1990** — Family memoir privately published. **PRIMARY FAMILY SOURCE / DOCUMENT ITSELF**
