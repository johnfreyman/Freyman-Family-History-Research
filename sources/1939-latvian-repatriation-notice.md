# 1939 Latvian Government Repatriation Notice

**Source:** *Valdības Vēstnesis*, no. 254, 8 November 1939  
**Repository:** Latvian National Library digital collection  
**URL:** https://dom.lndb.lv/data/obj/file/1374106.pdf

## Context

The issue contains official notices implementing the 30 October 1939 agreement concerning the transfer/repatriation of German-ethnicity Latvian citizens to Germany. It includes lists of persons processed through repatriation offices and notices concerning property of departing Germans.

## Ingrid entry

The issue lists:

**Ingrida (Ingrid) Freimanis (Freymann), born Deinhards**  
Born: **13 October 1911, Riga**  
Residence: **Zigfrīda Meierovica bulvāris 16, apartment 5, Riga**

The notice also lists two children with her; their exact birth dates are intentionally omitted from this public mirror for later-generation privacy. The private master archive preserves the complete transcription.

This independently corroborates George's memoir on Ingrid's identity and provides her exact birth date and a late-1939 Riga home address.

## Deinhardt lead

The same issue separately lists:

**Hedviga (Hedwig) Deinhards (Deinhardt), born Zālīte (Sahlit)**  
Born: **8 October 1883, Tērbata/Dorpat (Tartu)**  
Residence: **Ernestīnes iela 19, apartment 1, Riga**

This woman is an age-appropriate candidate for Ingrid's mother, especially because George says his mother-in-law accompanied Ingrid and the family during the 1939 resettlement. However, the government notice itself does **not** state a relationship between Hedwig and Ingrid.

**Current status:** HYPOTHESIS — do not enter as established mother/child relationship without another record.
