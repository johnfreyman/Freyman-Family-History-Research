# Research Handoff for ChatGPT

## Executive summary
State the most important breakthroughs, strongest evidence, major contradictions, and what remains unresolved.

## Findings

- **Person/entity:**

- **Direct URL:**
- **Date / page / record / archive reference:**
- **Evidence:**
- **Reasoning:**
- **Confidence:**
- **Contradictions / cautions:**
- **Recommended repo update:**

## Proposed relationships
Separate DOCUMENTED/CORROBORATED relationships from PROBABLE/HYPOTHESIS relationships.

## Timeline additions
Provide dated events suitable for `TIMELINE.md`.

## Corrections to existing research
Identify any repo claim that the evidence challenges. Quote or identify the existing claim precisely and explain why it should change.

## Negative searches and dead ends
Record archives, databases, query variants, names, dates, and collections searched unsuccessfully so another researcher does not repeat them unnecessarily.

## Unresolved questions
Rank the most valuable remaining questions.

## Recommended next actions
Give specific next searches/collections/records rather than generic advice.

## Source table
Rank sources by evidentiary strength and include stable URLs/identifiers.
