# Gemini Deep Research Assignment — Alice Brand Freymann

## Mission
Conduct a deep, multilingual investigation of **Alice Brand Freymann of Riga**, wife of Johannes/Hannes Ferdinand Freymann and mother of George E. Freymann. Build her ancestry, immediate family, life in Riga, and documentary footprint as far back as defensible evidence permits.

Read `AGENTS.md` first and obey its evidence rules.

## Established starting points
Treat these as research leads requiring independent verification unless the public repo provides documentary evidence:

- Family memoir identifies her as **Alice Brand of Riga**.
- Earlier family research gives dates **31 May 1883 – 25 Apr 1966**; independently verify them.
- Her mother reportedly died when Alice was about four.
- Her father reportedly remarried.
- Alice reportedly had a sister **Irma**.
- A paternal aunt **Olga**, a dressmaker, reportedly helped raise Alice and lived with a woman referred to as **Frl. Bogdanoff**.
- Alice reportedly worked as a clerk/cashier at **Bishof & Co.**, described in the memoir as a large Riga clothing store.
- Johannes/Hannes Freymann reportedly married Alice after returning from Siberia in 1908; locate the actual marriage record/date/place.
- Alice then traveled with Johannes across Russia to **Iman** in the Russian Far East, where son George was born in 1909.
- External maritime research indicates **Alice Freymann & M. Schnore, Riga** as owners of the former Dutch steamer *Groningen*, renamed *Alice* in 1926. Determine whether this is definitively the same Alice and seek original/contemporary ownership evidence.

## Primary questions
1. What were Alice Brand's exact birth/baptism details?
2. Who were her father and mother? Full names, maiden name, occupations, religion, residences, dates/places.
3. Can her mother's early death be documented?
4. Who was sister Irma? Trace her life if possible.
5. Who was paternal Aunt Olga? Establish full name and relationship from records rather than surname assumption.
6. Who was Frl. Bogdanoff and what was her relationship to Olga/Alice?
7. Can Bishof & Co. be identified in Riga directories/newspapers/business records? Was Alice employed there?
8. Locate Alice Brand + Johannes/Johann/Hannes Freymann's marriage record.
9. Search Riga address books for Brand household(s), Alice, Johannes, Olga, Irma, and Bishof & Co.
10. Determine church/confessional affiliation and search the appropriate Riga parish registers.
11. Trace the Brand ancestry at least one generation beyond Alice's parents where evidence allows.
12. Investigate Alice's legal/financial role in the 1926 ship *Alice*. Seek ship registers, ownership filings, maritime press, mortgage/insurance records, directories, and contemporary notices.
13. Search for Alice during Baltic German resettlement, wartime Germany/Gotenhafen, postwar Europe, and later life through 1966.
14. Search for photographs, obituaries, death notices, passenger/resettlement records, cemetery records, probate, naturalization/immigration material, and family-address evidence.
