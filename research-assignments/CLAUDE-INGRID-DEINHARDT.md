# Claude Opus Assignment — Ingrid Deinhardt and the Deinhardt Family

## Objective

Conduct a deep reconstruction of **Ingrid Deinhardt Freymann (born 13 Oct 1911, Riga)** and her immediate Deinhardt family using independent external evidence.

This is not a summarization task. The goal is to discover, verify, falsify, and document.

The original George & Ingrid Freymann memoir volumes are preserved in the private master archive and are **not public**. Memoir-derived claims in this public repo are leads and must remain labeled **PRIMARY FAMILY SOURCE** unless independently documented.

## Primary questions

1. Identify Ingrid's mother with documentary evidence.
2. Test the hypothesis that **Hedwig/Hedviga Deinhardt née Sahlit/Zālīte**, born 8 Oct 1883 in Tērbata/Dorpat (Tartu), was Ingrid's mother.
3. Identify Ingrid's father.
4. Locate Ingrid's **13 Oct 1911 Riga birth/baptism record**.
5. Locate the **4 June 1932 Riga marriage record** for Ingrid Deinhardt and George/Georg Freymann.
6. Reconstruct Ingrid's brothers **Wolf, Kurt, and Bernt Deinhardt**: full legal names, dates/places of birth, spouses, children, occupations, addresses, education/clubs, wartime movements, resettlement, and postwar lives.
7. Identify **Karin**, wife of Wolf Deinhardt, and the two children mentioned in the memoir.
8. Investigate the Deinhardt family's residence at or relationship to the **Riga Officers Club**.
9. Determine Ingrid's occupation/employer in Riga.
10. Determine whether the **Bernt Deinhardt** appearing in the 1961–62 history of the Baltischer Segler-Verband at Steinhuder Meer is Ingrid's brother.
11. Reconstruct the family's 1939 Baltic German resettlement and Gotenhafen household cluster.

## Priority repositories / source classes

Search the strongest available sources first, including where accessible:

- Latvian State Historical Archives / Raduraksti church and civil records.
- National Library of Latvia digital collections, periodicals, government notices, and Riga address books.
- Riga municipal/address directories and Baltic German Bürgerbücher/family collections.
- Estonian church/civil records and Tartu/Dorpat records for the Sahlit/Zālīte lead.
- German resettlement / Einwanderungszentralstelle (EWZ) indexes and files.
- Bundesarchiv references.
- Arolsen Archives where relevant.
- German address books and postwar directories.
- Sailing/rowing club histories and contemporary newspapers.
- Passenger, immigration, naturalization, and displaced-person records.
- Herder-Institut and other Baltic German genealogical collections.

Prefer actual records over snippets or derivative family trees.

## False-merge cautions

- Do not merge unrelated Deinhardt families from Germany merely because first names recur.
- Do not assume Sahlit/Zālīte ethnicity or kinship from surname form alone.
- Do not infer that a Dorpat/Tartu-born Hedwig is Ingrid's mother merely from age and surname.
- Do not assume the BSV Steinhude Bernt is Ingrid's brother until multiple identifiers align.
- Do not infer kinship from shared clubs, addresses, or profession alone.
