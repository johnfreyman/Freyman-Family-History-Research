## Evidence notes

- Georg Freymann, Marie Jaeger, their children, and Alice Brand are identified in George Freymann's memoir.
- Johannes Ferdinand Freymann's birth/death details are independently supported by the family grave monument and contemporary death notice found in earlier research.
- George gives his own birth as 20 September 1909 Old Style / 3 October 1909 Gregorian.
- A 1939 Latvian government repatriation notice documents Ingrid's birth as 13 October 1911 in Riga.
- SECOND HALF, Chapter 36 identifies Wolf, Kurt and Bernt as George's brothers-in-law, establishing them as Ingrid's brothers.
