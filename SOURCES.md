# Source Register

This public mirror records source descriptions and citations but intentionally does **not** expose private original family documents.

## Source ID policy

*Adopted 2026-09-05. This mirror copies IDs; it does not allocate them.*

1. **The private master's `SOURCES.md` is the only place that allocates a source ID.**
   This mirror copies them. It never assigns a new number.
2. **IDs are never reused.** An ID stays with the source it was first given to, permanently,
   even if that source is later withdrawn, superseded or reclassified.
3. **The ID carries no meaning.** Source type and evidence class are fields inside the entry,
   never encoded in the ID.

## S001 — George & Ingrid Freymann family memoir, FIRST HALF

**Type:** Primary family source / retrospective memoir  
**Format:** Scanned PDF, 76 pages  
**Authors:** George and Ingrid Freymann  
**Private publication:** 1990  
**Status:** Preserved in private master archive; active extraction

### Known relevant sections

- Chapter 4 — “Rote Duna”: Georg Freymann, Marie Jaeger, their children, Johannes/Hannes, Alice Brand.
- Chapter 5 — “Father John's Early Years”: schooling, sea career, the East Asiatic Company bark *Malaya*.
- Chapter 6 — “Port Arthur”: Johannes as dredging master; the Russo-Japanese war.
- Chapter 7 — “Roots to Siberia, Brewery”: the “Germania” brewery at Iman.
- Chapter 8 — “Wedding Bells”: Johannes's return to Riga, courtship/marriage, Alice's childhood and employment, move to Iman.
- Chapter 9 — “My Early Years”: George's dual-calendar birth date, brother Vanitschka, childhood in Iman.
- Chapter 11 — “Tsushima”: the 1917 transfer of the brewery; the move to Vladivostok.
- Chapters 12–17 — Vladivostok, the 1919 exodus, Shanghai, Hong Kong, and the 1925 voyage to Europe.
- Chapter 26 — “Bliss”: George and Ingrid's courtship and engagement.
- Chapter 27 — married life and family.
- Later chapters include shipping/business material.

### Reliability note

Extremely valuable because George and Ingrid describe their own lives and close relatives, but many events were written decades after they occurred. Treat as primary family testimony and seek contemporary corroboration.

## S003 — Family History Launch Point

**Type:** Research handoff compiled 29 Aug 2026  
**Status:** Baseline secondary research document, private master archive

### Major findings carried forward

- Johannes Ferdinand Freymann: born 24 Nov 1874 Riga; died 16 Aug 1941 Gotenhafen.
- Grave monument identifies him as `Reeder` (shipowner/shipping proprietor).
- Contemporary death notice identifies Johann Freymann as captain and co-owner of Latvian Shipping Co.
- Maritime sources connect Freymann with Oswald/Osvalds Andersons, Jānis Salcmans/Zalcmanis, and Kārlis Jansons in overlapping ownership groupings.

## S004 — John George Freyman, Sr., `The JGF Family Story`

**Type:** **PRIMARY FAMILY SOURCE** / first-person retrospective memoir  
**Author:** John George Freyman, Sr.  
**Identity in manuscript:** born Riga, 22 Mar 1933; first son of George Eduard Freyman/Freymann and Ingrid Deinhardt Freymann  
**Status:** Preserved in the private master archive; systematic extraction pending

John gives first-person testimony on Johannes/John Ferdinand Freyman, the family shipping business, the Aa/Lielupe-area home, the 1939 departure, his maternal grandmother travelling with the family, Ingrid's brothers Bernt and Kurt, Captain Schnore, wartime experience, immigration to America, and later Alaska fisheries work. He explicitly distinguishes `my father's book` from `my mother's book`, an important provenance clue.

### Reliability note

Written decades after the events. Treat as family testimony and seek contemporary corroboration. Agreement with another family memoir is **not** independent corroboration when both accounts may derive from the same family tradition.

## Citation convention

Research files should cite source IDs (`S001`, `S002`, etc.) plus enough detail to relocate the evidence: chapter/page where available, archival collection, newspaper/date, URL, or record identifier.

IDs are allocated only in the private master and copied here. Do not invent an ID in this repository.

Where an original family source is omitted from the public mirror for privacy, researchers should treat the markdown summary as a lead and clearly label it **PRIMARY FAMILY SOURCE** rather than pretending to have inspected the private document.
